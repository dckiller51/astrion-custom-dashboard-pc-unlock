Astrion PC Unlock

An ESP32-S3 plugged into your PC as a USB keyboard, that types your Windows PIN when the Astrion remote asks. The PIN stays on the ESP.

1. Install, in Chrome or Edge on a computer:

  1. M5Stack AtomS3U: hold its reset button about 2 seconds until the small green LED lights (download mode), then plug it into this computer.
    Another ESP32-S3: hold BOOT while plugging its USB port (not UART/COM).
  2. Click your board, pick the port, install. Then unplug it.
Your browser can't flash: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over HTTPS.

2. Set it up: plug it into the gaming PC. Its LED turns purple. From a phone, connect to the Astrion-Unlock-xxxx Wi-Fi and open http://192.168.4.1:

  1. Your Wi-Fi network and its password.
  2. Your Windows PIN (kept on the ESP only) and the PC's keyboard layout.
  3. Note the code for the remote (6 digits), then Save.

3. In Astrion: the remote's web page › Devices › your PC with Playnite › 🔓 Unlock ESP › its IP address (in your router's list, name astrion-unlock) and the code.

Then picking a game while the PC sleeps wakes it, unlocks Windows and starts the game.

Source and guide: github.com/dckiller51/astrion-custom-dashboard-pc-unlock
